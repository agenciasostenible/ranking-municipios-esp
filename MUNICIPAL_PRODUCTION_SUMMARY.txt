================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION
   - Jaén dominates Spain (37% of national production)
   - Top 3 provinces (Jaén, Córdoba, Sevilla) = 72% of national output
   - Within each province, 1-2 municipalities often drive 30-50% of DOP total

2. FRAGMENTATION VS. CONCENTRATION
   - Montes de Granada: Extreme fragmentation (54 municipalities, all small)
   - Baena/Estepa: High concentration (7-14 municipalities, clear leaders)
   - Antequera/Poniente: Moderate (16-18 municipalities, mixed sizes)

3. QUALITY vs. QUANTITY TRADE-OFF
   - Baena: ~40-57K tons/year, 2020 #1 AOVE commercialization globally
   - Priego: Only 11-16K tons/year but 450+ awards
   - Estepa: 38K tons with >80% AOVE category
   - Montes Granada: 25-35K tons spread over 54 municipalities (diluted per-capita)

4. CLIMATE VOLATILITY
   - Sierra Mágina: 22% year-over-year variation (normal)
   - Jaén/Granada: 83-116% swings between good/poor years (2024/25 vs. 2023/24)
   - Climate change evident in municipal variation (Bedmar/Jimena 50% drops)

5. DOP COVERAGE RATES
   - Córdoba: 52% of provincial production under DOP (140K of 271K)
   - Jaén: 20% of provincial production under DOP (88K of 445K)
   - Sevilla: 30% of provincial production under DOP (38K of 125K)
   - Granada: 50% of provincial production under DOP (45-60K of 103K)
   - Málaga: 100%+ of production under DOP (overlap/transfers)

RESEARCH SOURCES & METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Primary Sources:
  1. MAPA (Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación)
     - Aforos de producción (campaign estimates/finals)
     - Balances de mercado (annual reports)
     - AICA data (monthly production/inventory)

  2. Junta de Andalucía (Agencia de Gestión Agraria y Pesquera)
     - Aforo de Producción de Olivar (annual campaign estimates)
     - Provincial agricultural office reports

  3. DOP Regulatory Councils (Consejos Reguladores)
     - Official pliegos de condiciones (municipality lists)
     - Annual production statements (many published)
     - Membership registries (mills, farmers)

  4. Asoliva (Spanish Olive Oil Industry & Export Association)
     - DOP/IGP directory and contacts
     - Market analysis by designation

  5. Press releases & agricultural news:
     - Regional newspapers (Hora Jaén, Extra Jaén, etc.)
     - Agricultural websites (Oleumxauen, OleRrevista, AgroInformación)
     - University studies (Cátedra de Aceites de Jaén)

Methodology:
  - Aggregated confirmed DOP-level production totals from official sources
  - Cross-referenced municipality lists from pliego de condiciones
  - Estimated municipal distribution using:
    * Proportional hectares (total DOP hectares ÷ municipalities)
    * Known dominant municipalities (Baena, Estepa, Loja, Beas)
    * Historical patterns and press reports
    * Producer density and mill locations
  - Provincial figures verified against MAPA & Junta de Andalucía data
  - Campaign comparisons to assess volatility and trends

Limitations:
  - No official municipal production databases exist for most DOPs
  - Confidentiality restrictions on individual mill/farm data
  - Estimates require assumptions about distribution
  - Data lag: official figures published 6-12 months after campaign
  - Undesignated production poorly tracked (50-70% of provincial output)
